OUR STORY

Empahleni to the world. 🇿🇦

Mpahlas-Burg is more than clothing. It represents confidence, creativity, culture and the streets that shaped us.

From Poortjie to everywhere, we are building a brand that speaks for itself.

Mpahlasburg started in 2023 with an idea, a love for the streets and a simple belief: township style deserves to be seen everywhere.

We are township, people, culture, fashion, no limits. Every piece we make carries the energy of where we come from.

We are still growing, and every order, share and fit photo moves us forward. Thank you for rocking Mpahlasburg. 💧

FAQ's

How do I order?

Add your items to the cart, choose your colour and size, then tap checkout. Your order opens in WhatsApp and we confirm it with you there.

Can I pay by card online?

Right now orders are confirmed on WhatsApp. Card payments are coming soon, so keep an eye on this page.

How long does delivery take, and what does it cost?

Delivery time and cost depend on where you are. Message us on WhatsApp with your area and we'll give you the details when you order.

What if my size is wrong?

Not sure which size to pick? Message us on WhatsApp before you order and we'll help. If something doesn't fit after you receive it, contact us and we'll sort it out.

What is the 2 for R600 deal?

It's our limited tees deal. It's a limited drop, so once the stock is gone, it's gone. Order it from the Limited section on WhatsApp.

Do you have a physical store?

Not yet. For now we are online only, and you can order through the website or WhatsApp.

How can I contact you?

Message us on WhatsApp, or follow us on Facebook and TikTok.
