REFUND & RETURNS POLICY

We want you to love what you ordered. If something is not right, this is how we put it right.

Returns for size or fit

  • You may return an item within 14 days of receiving it if the size does not fit, or it is too big or too small.
  • The item must be unworn, unwashed and in its original condition, with its tags and packaging.
  • You can choose an exchange for another size, subject to stock, or a refund. Limited-edition items may sell out, so if your size is no longer available we will refund you instead.
  • For size returns, the customer pays the cost of sending the item back to us.

Faulty or damaged items

  • If your item arrives with a factory fault or damage, contact us as soon as you notice it. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act cover defective goods for up to 6 months after delivery.
  • Please send us clear photos of the fault together with your order number.
  • For faulty or damaged items we will repair, replace or refund the item, and we cover the return shipping cost.

How to make a return

  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order number, the item, and the reason for the return.
  • We will confirm your return and tell you where to send the item. Please do not send items back before we confirm.
  • Keep your proof of postage until we confirm that we received your parcel.

Refunds

  • Once we receive and check your return, we will refund you within 7 working days, to the same payment method you used or by EFT.
  • Original delivery fees are refunded only when the return is due to a fault, damage or an error on our side.

Items we cannot take back

  • Items that are worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
  • Items returned without their tags or without proof of purchase.
Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

SHIPPING POLICY

Where we deliver

We deliver to all 9 provinces of South Africa. We do not deliver outside South Africa at this time.

Delivery time

  • Orders are delivered within 7 to 10 working days after your payment is confirmed.
  • Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.
  • Delivery times to remote areas can take a little longer. We will tell you if that applies to your order.
  • Busy periods, such as sale and holiday seasons, may cause delays.

Delivery cost

The delivery fee depends on your area. We confirm the fee with you on WhatsApp before you pay, so there are no surprise costs.

Order confirmation and tracking

  • Your order is confirmed with our team on WhatsApp.
  • You receive an order number. Use it in the order tracker on our website, or message us on WhatsApp for an update.

Delivery address

  • Please give us a complete and correct delivery address and a phone number where you can be reached.
  • If a parcel is returned because of a wrong address or because nobody collected it, you may need to pay for it to be delivered again.

Receiving your parcel

  • Please check your parcel when it arrives. If it is damaged, or the wrong item was sent, contact us within 48 hours and send photos.
  • Ownership of the goods passes to you when they are delivered to the address you gave us.

Delays outside our control

We are not responsible for delays caused by things we cannot control, such as courier delays, weather, strikes or power cuts. We will keep you informed if your order is delayed.

PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it and how we look after it, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, phone number, delivery address and the items you order, which you send to us on WhatsApp.
  • Messages: anything you send us when you contact us, for example about a return.
  • Your cart: the items you add to your cart are saved in your own browser on your device so the cart is still there when you come back. This stays on your device.
  • Basic technical information: like most websites, the services that host and load this website (such as GitHub Pages and Google Fonts) may see technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

Why we collect it

  • To process, deliver and follow up on your order.
  • To handle returns, refunds and questions.
  • To keep our records as the law requires.
  • To keep you updated about promotions and new drops, only if you ask us to.

Who we share it with

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share only what is needed with delivery or courier companies so they can deliver your order, and with our payment provider when you pay online.
  • We may share information when the law requires it.

How we protect it

  • This website uses HTTPS, so information sent between your device and the website is encrypted.
  • We do not collect or store card details on this website.
  • Only authorised members of our team have access to order information, and our accounts are protected with two-step verification.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it for your order, our records and the law. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You may also object to us using your information for marketing. Message us on WhatsApp and we will respond as soon as we can.

If you believe we have not handled your information properly, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Our website is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place orders for you.
